I run a two-person studio, and I am almost always on my phone. Mobile is where NovaNote lost me completely. The web app is superb, full stop, but the mobile experience is a distant afterthought. Editing a table on my phone was painful, and the app dropped my offline changes twice. Losing work is the one thing I cannot tolerate.

I tried to give them the benefit of the doubt. I posted in the community, and other users confirmed the same lag with syncing and offline mode. The response from the team was positive and friendly, but the fixes never arrived during the six months I waited. Roadmap visibility is poor. There is a suggestion forum, but you cannot see which features are actually planned, and several highly voted requests were quietly closed.

Because I spend my day moving between the desk and the road, the desktop-only strength was not enough. I missed meetings because I could not open a reliable note on my phone. The pricing was fair and the desktop product excellent, but an unreliable mobile companion undermined everything.

When I finally migrated to a competitor, the export was quick and clean, which surprised me pleasantly. NovaNote handled leaving better than it handled my daily mobile use.

My summary in one line: beautiful on the desktop, fragile in my pocket. If they ever rebuild the mobile app with true offline reliability and surface their roadmap publicly, I would happily come back.
