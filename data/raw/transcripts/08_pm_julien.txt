We are a fifteen-person fintech team, and security was the entire conversation. I chose NovaNote because a colleague vouched for the product, and the reviews were strong. The editor is excellent and the writing experience is honestly wonderful. But for a financial company, the security posture was not formal enough.

There is single sign-on only on the most expensive plan, and even then identity policies are limited. We could not enforce granular retention, and audit logs are shallow. When our compliance officer asked for a export of access logs for a specific folder, the export omitted key events. That is a serious problem in our industry.

Data residency was the deal breaker. Our company policy requires data to remain in the region. NovaNote routed our workspace to a data center we could not select, and support said residency selection was restricted to certain tiers. We could not compromise on that, so the decision was effectively made for us.

The performance was good and onboarding was pleasant, but none of that matters when the product cannot meet regulatory requirements. I will also note that pricing transparency is poor. The features on each plan are unclear until you contact sales, which added friction to an already difficult decision.

We migrated everything to a self-hosted solution with full audit control. It was painful but non-negotiable. My advice to NovaNote is to lead with security maturity documents, clear residency options and honest plan feature tables, because that is what companies like ours need before anything else.
